I’m currently a designer at [Thinking Machines Lab](https://thinkingmachines.ai/). I also teach computer science and product design at [Parsons](https://www.newschool.edu/parsons/faculty/eric-li/).

With over 10 years of experience building products, my focus on design led engineering and product has allowed me to make significant impact at organizations like [Notion](https://www.notion.com/), the [Eames Institute](https://www.eamesinstitute.org/), [MoMA](https://www.moma.org/), [Google](https://design.google/), and [IDEO](https://ideo.com/).
